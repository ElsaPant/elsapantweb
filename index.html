Veta the Fluff

by ELSΔ PΔNT

Copyright © VetaTheFluff

VIEW PROJECT

Veta the Fluff is an original story and personal creative project celebrating kindness, friendship, love, and the simple beauty of everyday life. Through Veta, a fluffy sheep, and her adorable friends, the project shares uplifting little life lessons and the message that with a kind heart and by staying united, everything is possible.
The complete story is being written and illustrated by me as an illustrated book, while the 2D animation will extend Veta’s world through a story trailer and a series of animated shorts featuring heartwarming everyday moments, kindness, and friendship.
I’m developing the project from concept to completion, including story development, writing, art direction, character design, illustration, background art, storyboarding, animatics, 2D animation, motion, and final composition.

PROMETHEUS:

Prometheus: A proactive decision-making framework, by Stavros K. Stavroglou (University of Edinburgh), Athanasios A. Pantelous (Monash University), H. Eugene Stanley (Boston University), Bilal M. Ayyub, James H. Lambert.
Subtle animated video portraying the life of Prometheus, inspired by the visual style of ancient Greek vase paintings, with minimal movement designed to bring the artwork to life while maintaining its traditional aesthetic. The video played an important role in explaining why the scientific paper was called Prometheus and served as a link between art and science.
